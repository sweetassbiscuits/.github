Sip & Savor

Refined, yet inviting

Welcome to Cocoa Vino, a refined yet inviting wine bar where sophistication meets soulful ambiance. Nestled in the heart of the community, we specialize in rare and hard-to-find wines from California's premier regions, with standout selections from Napa Valley, Paso Robles, and a rotating collection of vintage wines and fine Champagnes.

Whether you're a seasoned collector or just discovering your palate, our curated list offers something truly special, by the glass or bottle.

On weekends, enjoy the rhythm of live music and the sparkle of mimosas: the perfect backdrop for a leisurely afternoon or an elegant evening out.

A place to unwind, celebrate, and savor the moment.
