How I grade things

Every listing gets one of four grades, and every flaw gets named in plain words. If I can find it, you'll hear about it.

1Excellent

Looks like it barely left the house, with no flaws you'd notice without a magnifier.

2Very Good

Gently worn, with a small flaw or two that I name in the listing and you'd likely never notice in use.

3Good

Clearly lived a life, with wear you can see, but ready to wear today exactly as it is.

4Loved

Has real wear or damage that I spell out and price for, and it's still too good to leave behind.
