How drops work

New finds every Sunday. Here's the deal.

It's simple and it's fair to everybody. Here's how a drop goes, start to finish.

  1. Sun
    10am

    The list sees it first

    New finds go out to the email list on Sunday at 10am. Every piece, with photos, prices and flaws.

  2. Sun
    11am

    Then Instagram Stories

    An hour later, at 11am, the same finds go up on Instagram Stories for everyone else.

  3. First

    First to claim wins

    See something you love? Hit "Claim it." Whoever claims first gets it. That's the whole rule.

  4. No holds

    No holds, no exceptions

    I don't hold pieces, not even for my favorite people. It keeps things fair for all of you.

  5. 1 hour

    Invoice within the hour

    Once you claim it, I'll send your invoice within the hour. Pay it and it's yours. [HOW LONG BUYERS HAVE TO PAY]

Quick answers

What if I miss something I wanted?

Sold pieces stay on the site marked "Found a home." Tap "Tell me if one like it turns up" and I'll keep an eye out on my next hunt.

Can you hold something until payday?

Sorry, no holds. First to claim wins, every time. It's the only way to keep it fair.

Do you ever restock?

Never. Everything is one of one. When it's gone, it's gone, and then I go find the next thing.

How do I get on the list?

Pop your email in the box below. One email a week, Sunday at 10am, with every new find.
