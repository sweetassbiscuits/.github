Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026

The short version

We collect as little as possible. This site has no advertising trackers and no analytics cookies, and we never sell or share your information.

Newsletter

If you join our email list, we keep your email address and the interests you tick (events, wine club) so we can send you the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to delete your address at any time.

Maps

The map on our Visit page is provided by Google. It only loads if you choose to open it, at which point Google's own privacy policy applies.

Private event requests

If you ask about a private event, we use your name, email, phone and event details only to plan it with you.

Event tickets

Tickets and RSVPs are handled on our event pages. Anything you enter there is used only to manage your booking.
